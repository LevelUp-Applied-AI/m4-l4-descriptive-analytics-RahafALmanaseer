# Findings

## Observations
- GPA mostly between 2.5 and 3.5
- Students who study more tend to have higher GPA
- Internship students seem to perform better
- Attendance is generally high, but some students with low attendance may have lower GPA
- Study hours per week vary; more study often relates to better grades
- Some departments have slightly higher average GPA, but differences are small

## Tests
- T-test (internship vs GPA): t = 13.56, p ≈ 0.00 → significant difference. Students with internships have higher GPA on average.
- ANOVA (GPA by department): F = 0.67, p = 0.61 → no significant difference. GPA does not vary significantly between departments.

## Insights
- Encouraging students to take internships could improve overall academic performance
- Promoting consistent study habits is important for better GPA
- Monitoring attendance may help identify students at risk and provide support
