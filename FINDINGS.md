# Findings

## Observations
- GPA mostly between 2.5 and 3.5
- Students who study more hours per week tend to have higher GPA
- Internship students seem to perform better than those without internships
- Attendance is generally high, but some students with low attendance have lower GPA
- Study hours per week vary; more study often relates to better grades
- Some departments have slightly higher average GPA, but differences are small

## Tests
- T-test (internship vs GPA):
  - t = 13.56
  - p ≈ 0.00
  - Interpretation: Students with internships have higher GPA on average. The result is statistically significant.
- ANOVA (GPA by department):
  - F = 0.67
  - p = 0.61
  - Interpretation: No significant difference in GPA across departments. GPA does not vary significantly between departments.

## Insights
- Encouraging students to take internships could improve overall academic performance
- Promoting consistent study habits is important for better GPA
- Monitoring attendance may help identify students at risk and provide support
