# Findings

## Observations
- GPA mostly between 2.5 and 3.5
- Students who study more tend to have higher GPA
- Internship students seem to perform better

## Tests
- T-test (internship vs GPA): t = 13.56, p ≈ 0.00 → significant difference
- ANOVA (GPA by department): F = 0.67, p = 0.61 → no significant difference
